**Baselines.** Persistence (tomorrow equals today), climatology, ridge regression and a random forest run on the same days as the LSTM; the best by validation NSE is the number to beat. They are also the yardstick of every added input: ridge and forest receive the same new input, so a gain is attributed to {{num:fw_info_not_net}} when they gain as much (not run at {{num:e3_n_rivers}} training rivers).

**Ablation by information.** Information is added one input at a time: past weather alone; plus the river gauge [@feng2020]; plus NLDAS-2 rain to the evening; plus ERA5, then AORC, rain to local midnight ({{num:fw_aorc}}); plus the GEFS control-member forecast; plus the perfect rain, then the perfect weather, of the next three days, both {{num:fw_upper}}. Variants take one thing away (Table T6, Appendix C).

**The controls.** Each control differs from its test in one thing: "_older", the same series one day older (the issue day's hours removed, the second rain product kept); "_to09", the same product over the rain table's own window (ending {{num:day_end_daymet_rounded}} on the issue day), so test minus control is the worth of the later hours; "_clim", the forecast shuffled within basin and calendar month across years, which in networks trained on exact slots measures harm, so the clean control is the retrained shuffled set of the satellite-era split. The Stage IV substitution replaces the product, not the hours (Section 7.2).

Figure 1 is the project's data-flow drawing: from the {{num:n_products}} products through the basin audit, one daily table per basin and the split by target day to training, stored forecasts and scores. The technical route runs in five steps: raw data; preprocessing (basin means, clocks, scalers and the high-flow level fitted on the training years only); the baselines (persistence, climatology, ridge regression and a random forest, Section 5.4); the proposed model, one LSTM per input set trained with several seeds (Section 5.3); and the evaluation, the test years scored once. Decisions are fixed in advance: the basin rule; the settings, stopping point and warning level, all chosen on the validation years; the verdict criteria (GO at a POD of at least {{num:go_min_warned}} and a FAR of at most {{num:go_max_false}}). The feedback loop runs through the information, not the network: each NOT YET verdict led to the next rung of the ladder (Section 7.1), with its own control, re-scored on the same test years. There is no deployment stage (Appendix D).

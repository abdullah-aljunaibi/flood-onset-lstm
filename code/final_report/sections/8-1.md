**Reading.** In the models tried here, what the forecast knows at the issue time mattered more than the kind of network (Section 7.1): the simple models given the same new inputs gain as many onsets as the LSTM. This holds for {{num:n_basins}} humid basins, a daily step and one LSTM size (Section 8.2).

**Genuine contributions, to our knowledge.** First, each rain product's clock was measured, not assumed (the data papers note one-day offsets [@thornton2021], [@kratzert2021] but never price them), and its cost priced with matched-window controls: the same product over the table's window or the UTC day, and the same rain a day older. AORC rain to midnight ({{num:fw_aorc}}) raised the onsets warned from {{num:lstm_wq_l1_onset_pod}} to {{num:lstm_wqa_l1_onset_pod}} ({{num:fw_hindsight}}) while the matched control stayed at {{num:lstm_wqa_to09_l1_onset_pod}}. Second, a ladder of information up to a perfect forecast ({{num:fw_upper}}), judged by one pre-fixed warning rule where operational work reports return-period reliability [@nearing2024]. Third, a real-time substitution: Stage IV rain [@nelson2016] in place of the AORC input [@fall2023] on the onset issue days kept {{num:s4_stage4_effect_surviving}} of the gain ({{num:s4_stage4_share}}; {{num:fw_realtime}}).

**Routine integration.** One LSTM shared by basins with static properties follows [@kratzert2019], [@kratzert2024]; ridge and forest yardsticks, NSE [@nash1970], the warning scores and the Transformer comparison [@vaswani2017] are standard.

**Imagery.** The project classified and interpreted no optical satellite scene, so capture date and sun angle are not relevant. Three products derived from satellites were used as delivered and are named for what they are. IMERG rain merges passive-microwave and infrared estimates (NASA), and its daily value covers the UTC day. The elevation tiles are a composite in the SRTM format, built mainly from the SRTM radar mission, an active sensor; a tile does not record which source each cell came from. The Annual NLCD land-cover map of {{num:gis_land_cover_year}} was made by USGS from Landsat images, and its map year is the date that applies. The weather tables (Daymet, NLDAS, MERRA-2, ERA5, AORC, Stage IV, GEFS) are gauge analyses, radar and gauge analyses, reanalyses or forecasts, not images.
